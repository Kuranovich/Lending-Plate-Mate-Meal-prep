Our approach

The benefit is in the ingredients, portions and routine

We don’t promise miracles. We make eating well every day simple: clear ingredients, balanced portions and three meals a day.

01

Protein and clear macro targets

Every plan lists calories, protein, fat and carbs. Our dishes are built around protein sources — cottage cheese, chicken, duck, veal and fish. We don’t use pork. You know what you eat without counting or weighing.

02

Balanced portions and five calorie options

From 1250 to 2200 kcal a day. Portion sizes depend on the plan, so it’s easy to match your goal — losing, maintaining or gaining weight.

03

Full breakfast, lunch and dinner every day

Food for the whole day, not separate dishes. Regular meals help you build a calm routine without snacking on the go.

    FAQ

    What are the dishes made of?

    A sample day: cottage cheese muffins with fruit, veal meatballs with bulgur and baked sea bass with quinoa. Our dishes are based on cottage cheese, chicken, duck, veal and fish; we don’t use pork. The manager will send you the full ingredients of every dish before you order. The menu and portion sizes depend on your plan.

    Can you take allergies or foods I don’t eat into account?

    Please tell the manager about any allergies or restrictions before you order. They will tell you which ingredients the dishes contain and honestly say whether a suitable option is possible.

    How should I store the meals?

    Put your meals in the fridge right after delivery. Storage conditions and shelf life are on the packaging — if anything is unclear, the manager will help.

    How do I reheat the dishes?

    Hot dishes just need reheating in a microwave. Reheating instructions are on the packaging.

    How do I choose the calories?

    Use the calculator — it shows an approximate guideline and the closest plan. You can also pick a plan yourself, and if you’re unsure, the manager will help you decide.

    I have high cholesterol. Is the plan right for me?

    Plate Mate plans are balanced everyday food, not a therapeutic diet, and we don’t promise changes in test results. If you have recommendations from your doctor, share them with the manager: we’ll show you the ingredients so you can decide together with your doctor.

    How often do you deliver?

    Every two days, free of charge within Pattaya. The manager will agree the first delivery date and address with you.
